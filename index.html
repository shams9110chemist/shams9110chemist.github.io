About

I moved from QAU's chemistry classrooms into a GMP pharmaceutical QC lab, and now into materials research at COMSATS. Two years of HPLC, FTIR and documentation work taught me reproducibility; my MS thesis applies it to Co-doped WO₃@ZnO heterojunctions that degrade dyes under light. I have published on neem-mediated ZnO against multidrug-resistant bacteria, and I teach Cambridge science at Pak Turk Maarif, which keeps me explaining ideas clearly. I am looking for a PhD in functional nanomaterials for environmental and energy technologies.
